## Rock Paper Scissors

A simple Rock Paper Scissors game developed in C during my first year of Computer Engineering at EMSI.

🎮 About the Game

The player plays against the computer in a series of Rock Paper Scissors rounds.
At the beginning of the game, the player chooses how many points are needed to win.
The computer randomly chooses between:

Rock
Paper
Scissors

The player enters their choice, and the program determines the winner of each round.

- Loops

🛠️ Technologies

The objective of this project was to practice C programming fundamentals and algorithmic problem solving by developing a simple interactive game.
